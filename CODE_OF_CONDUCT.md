# Code of Conduct

This project and everyone who takes part in it follow the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). By taking part
(issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews) you agree to uphold it.

In short: be respectful, assume good faith, give and accept feedback about the work and not the person, and do not
harass anyone.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour privately through
[GitHub private vulnerability reporting](https://github.com/bishalg/create-TRAN/security/advisories/new), the channel
named in [SECURITY.md](./SECURITY.md); pick it even though the report is not a vulnerability, and say it is a conduct
report. Do not open a public issue. The maintainer reviews every report and keeps the reporter's identity confidential.
No separate email address is published.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or remove contributions, and warn, temporarily restrict or ban a person, following the
[enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines) of
the Covenant.
